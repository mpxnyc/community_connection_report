Person-place graph

Conceptually, the MPX NYC spatial participant data network is a network object that contains all collected data. In this network, nodes comprise of community districts and survey participants. Edges can only exist between a person and a communtiy district - they indicate that the person either lives in or has a contact place in that community district. In ?@fig-data, participant nodes are blue and community district nodes are pink. Table 1 shows the node and ?@tbl-example-bipartite-edges the edges of the network.
